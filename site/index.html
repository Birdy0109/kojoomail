Kojoo Mail

一个窗口，收所有邮箱

Kojoo Mail 是 Windows 上的桌面邮件客户端。Gmail、Outlook、QQ、网易、iCloud 和企业邮箱都能加进来，新邮件按时间排在一起；可选的 AI 帮你写摘要、提取待办、起草回复。

Kojoo Mail is a desktop email client for Windows. Add Gmail, Outlook, QQ, NetEase, iCloud and work mailboxes, read them in one unified inbox, and optionally let AI summarise messages, extract to-dos and draft replies.

数据在你电脑上

邮件只缓存在本机，密码和登录授权存在 Windows 凭据管理器。我们没有服务器，不收集任何数据。

Mail is cached only on your computer; credentials live in Windows Credential Manager. We run no servers and collect no data.

一键登录 Gmail

用谷歌官方登录页授权，密码只在谷歌页面输入。授权只用来收发你自己的邮件。

Sign in with Google's own consent page. Access is used only to send and receive your own mail.

AI 可选，用你自己的密钥

AI 只在你配置了自己的模型密钥后才工作，每个邮箱都能单独关掉。AI 只起草，从不替你发信。

AI works only with your own model API key, can be turned off per account, and never sends mail on your behalf.
